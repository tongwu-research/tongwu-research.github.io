# Research figure provenance

Only individual figures are included; the original application materials are not distributed with this website.

- `gaussian-system-card.jpg`: corrected two-row representative system artwork, copied unchanged from the existing 3000 × 2000 source. The homepage displays the complete image inside a centered 4:3 frame and links to the full-resolution original. The wrong-key example uses BER = 0.75; the earlier 800 × 600 export with BER approximately 0.5 is not used. No cropping or pixel edits are applied.
- `holography.png`: existing 768 × 768 baseline phase reconstruction of Siemens star and USAF test targets from the Warsaw/QCI research materials. Converted from its 8-bit TIFF to PNG with pixel identity verified. A centered SVG viewport shows a detail of one Siemens star; the image link opens the full original field. It illustrates the research subject and does not present a new-method comparison or quantitative result.
- `bench.png`: original embedded optical-apparatus image from Tong Wu's research presentation, slide 7, describing the Chiba University project. Existing apparatus labels are preserved.

The website portrait is the original-proportion outdoor photograph supplied by Tong Wu. No generative or face edits were applied.
